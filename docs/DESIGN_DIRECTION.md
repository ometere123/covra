# COVRA visual direction

## Product

COVRA is an evidence-first testnet assurance ledger for autonomous operations. It shows only protocol configuration, verified Base Sepolia state, Bradbury decisions and reconciled account records.

## Character

Exact, editorial, watchful.

## World

A serious financial publication crossed with a risk operator's control room: warm porcelain, quiet hairlines, restrained ink, and data treated as evidence rather than decoration.

## Moments

The landing page uses a protocol-state schematic. Workspace screens give visual emphasis to provenance timelines, chain state and empty-but-honest registers. Oxide red marks attention and failure; ultramarine marks verified network state.

## Signature

The source-of-truth rule: every meaningful figure is paired with where it came from, and unavailable state is shown as unavailable instead of being filled with illustrative activity.

## Constraints

- Carbon `#1D1D20`, warm porcelain `#F4F0E9`, ivory `#FFFCF7`, oxide red `#A63D32`, ultramarine `#3458A4`, slate `#68717F`, and warm stone `#D8D1C7` are the starting palette.
- No green, lemon, lime, chartreuse, hue gradients, glows, decorative blockchain imagery or fabricated operational data.
- Typography uses a quiet sans interface with a restrained editorial serif for large display moments only.
- Status is communicated by text, icon and a small semantic mark, never by color alone.

The craft principles in this pass were informed by [Unslop](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop) by Damir Sokolovsky, CC BY 4.0. This document is an original COVRA direction, not a copy of that work.
